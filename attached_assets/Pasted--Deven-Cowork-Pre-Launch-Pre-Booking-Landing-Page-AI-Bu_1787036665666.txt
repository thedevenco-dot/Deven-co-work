# Deven Cowork — Pre-Launch Pre-Booking Landing Page
## AI Build Prompt

Paste this into your AI builder (Antigravity / Stitch / Replit Agent) to reframe the existing landing page into a pre-launch pre-booking page.

## CONTEXT FOR THE AI

You are rebuilding the landing page for **Deven Cowork**, a 50-seat premium coworking space opening soon in City Centre, Raipur, Chhattisgarh. The space is **not open yet** — this page exists to convert early interest into **reserved, deposit-backed pre-bookings** before launch day, so the space opens already full or close to full.

This is NOT a "book a tour" page. It is a "reserve your seat before we open" page. Every section, headline and CTA must reflect that the doors aren't open yet — this is the founding batch, and being early is the entire value proposition.

**Critical rules to bake into copy, pricing logic and the booking form:**
- Total capacity: **50 seats**
- Maximum **7 seats per client / founder / company** — no single business can block-book the whole space. State this plainly wherever seat count is discussed (pricing, FAQ, form) so multi-seat enquirers know the ceiling upfront instead of finding out after submitting.
- This is a **pre-booking**, not a completed sale — frame it as "reserve with a refundable deposit," not "buy now."
- Once the space opens, every member gets a **2-day free trial**, then a **7-day "love it or leave it" window with a 100% refund** if they walk away — no conditions, no fine print. This is the single strongest trust asset on the page and should headline the risk reversal section, not the deposit-refund line alone.

---

## BRAND SYSTEM (carry over, do not redesign)
- Primary brand colour: **#FFC247** (amber/yellow) — reserved exclusively for CTA buttons and one accent use per section, nowhere else
- Base: dark **#0D0D0D**
- Typography: **Bebas Neue** for display/headlines, **DM Sans** for body
- Mobile-first: 80–90% of traffic will be mid-range Android on 4G. Design the mobile stack first, desktop is the adaptation.
- No stock photography. If final space photography isn't ready yet, use render/concept images or in-progress build photos labelled honestly ("Space currently being finished — here's a preview") rather than pretending it's complete.
- One CTA button colour, used nowhere else on the page. CTA text must be a first-person action verb: "Reserve My Seat" — never "Submit," "Learn More," or "Enquire Now."
- Maximum 4 form fields: Name, Phone, Seats needed (dropdown capped at 7), Preferred plan (Hot Desk / Dedicated Desk / Cabin / Virtual Office)

## PAGE STRUCTURE — BUILD IN THIS ORDER

### 1. Sticky Header
Logo (left) · Phone number, tappable (right) · CTA button "Reserve My Seat"
No full navigation menu — this is a landing page, not the main site. Every link is an exit.

### 2. Hero
**Job:** In under 5 seconds — what this is, where it is, that it opens soon, what it costs, and how to reserve.
- Headline formula: Outcome + Price + Constraint + Pre-launch framing
  - e.g. *"Raipur's most beautiful office. Opening soon. First 50 seats — reserve yours now."*
- Sub-headline: one sentence stating the location, the studio/growth differentiator, and the seat cap
  - e.g. *"A dedicated desk in the centre of the city, with a content studio and growth team attached. Only 50 seats total, maximum 7 per business."*
- Primary CTA: "Reserve My Seat"
- Trust strip under the button: *"2-day free trial · 7-day 100% refund · Refundable deposit · Opening [Month]"*
- Visual: hero render/progress photo or looping build-progress clip, muted, static-image fallback on slow connections. CTA must be visible without scrolling on a 360px viewport.

### 3. Trust Bar
"Built by DEVEN — the agency behind [X] Raipur brands" line · Founder names/photos · Location line with a recognisable landmark · Opening timeline stated plainly (e.g. "Opening [Month Year]")
Since there's no live GMB review history yet, lead with **founder and agency credibility**, not review counts.

### 4. Problem — Named, Not Invented
Three short, specific pain blocks (not generic):
1. A client asks where your office is — and you change the subject.
2. Your best work happens between a doorbell, a delivery and the television in the next room.
3. You look smaller on a video call than your business actually is.
Keep brief — over-agitation reads as manipulation.

### 5. The Reframe
Headline: *"This is not a coworking space."*
Body: position Deven Cowork as a workspace + content studio + growth team, not a chair rental. Give this section visual weight — dark background, generous spacing. This is the strategic centre of the page.

### 6. Why Reserve Before We Open
**New section specific to pre-launch.** Explain what early reservers get that later joiners won't:
- Founding pricing locked for as long as they stay, even after rates rise post-launch
- First choice of desk / cabin location once the space opens
- Priority studio booking slots in month one
- Refundable deposit — no risk in reserving early
Frame this as the entire reason to act now instead of waiting for the doors to open.

### 7. The Offer Stack
Two-column table: inclusion (left) → rupee value (right). Total value at bottom, actual price beneath it.
Include: Dedicated desk 24×7 access · Unlimited Coffee Lab · Meeting room hours/month · Monthly studio reel · GMB setup · Monthly growth session · GST business address · Raipur Founders feature · Member network
Bold the 3 items no competitor offers.

### 8. Space Preview
Renders / build-progress photography with honest captions (carry information, not adjectives — "Third-floor windows on two sides" beats "beautiful light"). If real photos aren't ready, say so once, briefly, then move on — don't dwell on it.
CTA immediately below this section.

### 9. The Differentiator — Studio & Growth
Own section, not a bullet. What a member gets monthly from the content studio + growth session. Frame as an outcome the buyer already wants but hasn't solved.

### 10. Pricing & Seat Reservation
Three columns: Hot Desk / Dedicated Desk (highlighted, "Most Popular") / Private Cabin. Monthly/quarterly/annual toggle. Show annual saving in rupees, not percentage.
**Directly beneath the pricing table, state the seat rule clearly:**
> *"50 seats total for our founding batch. Maximum 7 seats per client or founder — this keeps the space diverse and every member gets real access to the community, not just a desk."*
This must appear here and again in the FAQ and the booking form, not buried in one place.

### 11. Risk Reversal
This section carries two stacked guarantees — lead with the trial/refund policy, since it's the stronger, more concrete promise; the deposit-refund line supports it underneath.
> *"2 days, free. Then 7 days to decide — love it or leave it, 100% refunded."*
> *"Walk in for your first two days at no cost. Keep working here for a week. If Deven Cowork isn't the best place you've worked in Raipur, leave — and get every rupee back. No conditions, no fine print."*

Beneath that, restate the pre-launch deposit guarantee for reservers:
> *"Reserving now? Your deposit is fully refundable if you change your mind before we open, or if your 7-day trial doesn't work out once we do."*

Full-width band, high contrast, impossible to scroll past. This section should do more conversion work than any other block on the page — give it generous space.

### 12. Scarcity & Urgency
- Seats remaining out of 50 (real, honestly updated — never a fake counter)
- The 7-seat-per-client cap restated as a reason to reserve now rather than assume you can add seats later
- Opening date / deadline for founding pricing
> *"23 of 50 founding seats reserved. Founding pricing disappears the day we open."*
Never use a countdown timer that resets.

### 13. FAQ — Objection Handling
Accordion, FAQ schema. Must include pre-launch-specific questions:
- When exactly do you open?
- Is my deposit really refundable, and how do I get it back?
- Can my company reserve more than 7 seats?
- What happens to my price if I reserve now vs. after launch?
- Is there a lock-in period once we open?
- Can I use this address for GST registration?
- What if the opening date shifts?
- Do I get to choose my desk/cabin, or is it assigned?
- How does the 2-day free trial actually work — do I pay anything upfront?
- If I take the 7-day refund, do I get my reservation deposit back too, or just the membership fee?
- Is there a catch to the "love it or leave it" refund — do I need a reason?
Plus the standard operational ones (parking, hours, meeting rooms).

### 14. Location Proof
Map embed · drive times from known landmarks · exterior/build-progress photo · parking detail.

### 15. Final CTA
Offer restated in 3 lines · Reservation form (Name, Phone, Seats needed [capped at 7], Preferred plan) · WhatsApp alternative button · Phone number.
Directly under the seats field, a one-line reminder: *"Maximum 7 seats per business."*
Form fully visible without a click-through popup.

## COPY RULES
- Same CTA text everywhere: **"Reserve My Seat"**
- State the low commitment beside the button: "refundable," "2 minutes," "no card required upfront" (adjust based on actual deposit mechanics)
- A CTA block after every third section
- Never use "Learn More," "Click Here," "Enquire Now," or "Get Started"
- Every claim gets visible evidence beside it — a render, a founder photo, a number. No unsupported claims.
- Real scarcity only — 50 is a physical fact, 7-per-client is a real policy. State both plainly and never inflate or fake either number.

## TECHNICAL / CONVERSION NON-NEGOTIABLES
- Floating WhatsApp button, bottom-right, every screen, thumb-reachable, pre-filled message referencing pre-booking
- Sticky header CTA visible on scroll, mobile and desktop
- Form submits to CRM via webhook with an instant WhatsApp/SMS auto-reply within 60 seconds
- Thank-you page confirms: seat reserved pending deposit / deposit received, next steps, expected opening date
- Mobile load under 3 seconds on simulated 4G, tap targets 44px minimum, body text 16px minimum, no horizontal scroll
- No fake urgency, no member login portal, no online membership checkout (deposit collection only, full membership payment happens closer to/at opening)

---

## WHAT NOT TO BUILD IN THIS PASS
- Real-time seat availability map
- Multi-language toggle
- Full membership checkout (deposit/reservation only)
- Elaborate animations that slow mid-range Android load times
- A live chat bot — WhatsApp is the chat channel
